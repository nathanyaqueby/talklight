          Privacy first

          TalkLight measures

          • How long you speak
          • How loud you speak (one volume number)

          TalkLight never

          • Records or stores audio
          • Understands or stores your words
          • Reads emotions
          • Sends data anywhere or sells it

          On your device

          No account, no tracking, no server. Only your role, settings and calendar entries are saved in this browser, and you can delete them in Settings.

          About TalkLight

          Pause your voice, not your ideas.

          TalkLight is a voice wellbeing companion for people who talk for a living: teachers, university lecturers, trainers and presenters. It tracks speaking time and loudness, never words, and gives a silent reminder before your voice gets tired.
